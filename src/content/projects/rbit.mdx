**Simulated battery:** 200 MW / 400 MWh, 90% round-trip efficiency, $10/MWh degradation cost. Price taker, energy market only.

**Agent:** dueling double DQN with noisy-network exploration and an n-step replay buffer.

<RbitCharts part="charts" />

The price axis is clipped at the 99th percentile. Times are NEM time (AEST).

## Results

<RbitCharts part="summary" />

The agent captured 33% (TAS1) to 64% (QLD1) of perfect-foresight profit, cycling about once a day.

## Method

Each interval the agent picks charge, hold or discharge at full power, from price, demand and interconnector lags, AEMO's 5-minute (P5) and pre-dispatch reports, and its state of charge. The benchmark is a linear program over the whole period. Data: AEMO's NEMweb.
