**Simulated battery:** 200 MW / 400 MWh, 90% round-trip efficiency, $10/MWh degradation cost. Price taker, energy market only.

A deep reinforcement-learning agent charges and discharges it every five minutes on Australia's electricity market, seeing only data published beforehand. Forward test: 1 September to 6 October 2026, five regions.

<RbitCharts part="charts" />

The price axis is clipped at the 99th percentile. Times are NEM time (AEST).

## Results

<RbitCharts part="summary" />

The agent captured 33% (TAS1) to 64% (QLD1) of perfect-foresight profit, cycling about once a day. Perfect foresight is the most the same battery could earn knowing every price in advance.

## Method

Dueling double DQN with noisy-network exploration. Each interval it picks charge, hold or discharge at full power, from price, demand and interconnector lags, AEMO's 5- and 30-minute forecasts, and its state of charge. The benchmark is a linear program over the whole period. Data: AEMO's NEMweb.

## Limitations

- 36 days in one season; other conditions could look quite different.
- The benchmark must end with an empty battery, which inflates "% of perfect" slightly (not measured).
- Flat degradation cost, no network charges, no frequency-control revenue, no price impact. Simulated, not a real battery.
- The daily job has stopped; these numbers are a fixed snapshot.
