## Limitations

- Mine is always 1 to 1.5 points lower than AEMO's. Small non-scheduled generation is not counted here, which I think explains the gap but have not confirmed.
- AEMO's half-hour record on 6 Nov 2024 was 75.6%; mine is 73.8%, probably for the same reason.
- Rooftop solar is an estimate. I use only the `MEASUREMENT` series, because summing it with the satellite series overcounts two to three times. I have not checked AEMO's documentation on which series is preferred.
- AEMO's data has gaps, for example 5 Sep 2024, when 34 of about 470 units were missing for one half hour. Records skip intervals with under 90% of the usual unit count.
- "Gas and other" is gas, small other fuels and the under 1% of generation, by energy, from units I could not map to a fuel.
## Data sources

All from AEMO's public NEMweb archive, July 2018 to now, for the five mainland NEM regions:

- Unit-level output (SCADA) for every scheduled and semi-scheduled generator: wind, utility solar, hydro, coal, gas.
- AEMO's rooftop solar estimate (`ROOFTOP_PV_ACTUAL`, the `MEASUREMENT` series).
- AEMO's generator registration list, to map each unit to a fuel. A small override file covers units missing from it.

Renewables are utility wind, utility solar, rooftop solar and conventional hydro; pumped storage and batteries are excluded. Share is renewables divided by scheduled and semi-scheduled generation plus rooftop, from the 30-minute data. Charts show the 12-month rolling average.
